FAQ

AI meeting reports: common questions

What is an AI meeting report?

An AI meeting report is a structured result generated from a meeting recording. LOREVI creates an Executive Slide and Interactive Web Report with key metrics, decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, risks and insights.

Can AI create a meeting report from a recording?

Yes. Upload meeting audio or video to LOREVI. The service analyzes the conversation and turns it into a structured meeting result.

How is LOREVI different from meeting transcription?

Transcription records what was said. LOREVI turns the meeting into an executive-ready result with an Executive Slide, key metrics and an interactive report you can edit and share.

Does LOREVI identify action items and owners?

Yes. When tasks, responsibilities and deadlines are discussed, LOREVI identifies and structures them in the report.

Can I edit the AI-generated meeting report?

Yes. The Interactive Web Report can be edited after the meeting has been processed.

Can I share a meeting report with my team?

Yes. You can share the Web Report with colleagues or export the result as an Executive PDF.

Does LOREVI keep the meeting transcript?

The transcript remains available in the report as supporting context for the structured meeting result.
